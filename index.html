Core Scope of VIXM

Capturing end-to-end ground & approach dimensions for vertiports

Static Infrastructure

TLOF/FATO geometry, obstacle clearance surfaces (OIS), passenger gates, identification, and safety constraints.

Dynamic Operations

Real-time pad availability, charging/MRO station status, slot reservations, and temporary operating restrictions.

SWIM Integration

Full semantic interoperability with ATM & UTM architectures via standardized XML (GML) and JSON payloads.
